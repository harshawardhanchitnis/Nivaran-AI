Agentic, with guardrails

The AI investigates. Code decides. You approve.

The agent chooses its own next action from a small set of tools, but anything that could mislead you is handled by code or by you.

psychology

What the AI does

  • checkReads documents and copies the exact quote for each fact
  • checkChooses its next step: look again, ask you, search the rules, propose a plan
  • checkWrites the wording of the plan and the complaint
rule

What code decides

  • checkWhether each quote really appears in the document
  • checkConflicts, dates, deadlines and the escalation step
  • checkEvery amount, date and ID in the complaint, inserted from your evidence
block

What it can never do

  • closeSend, file, pay or delete anything
  • closeCite a rule a person has not checked
  • closeSee the name and contact details you type into the complaint

What Nivaran covers

  • checkAn online order you paid for and then cancelled
  • checkA return the seller accepted or picked up
  • checkA refund the seller confirmed in writing

What it does not cover

  • closeBank or UPI transfer disputes
  • closeArguments over whether a refund is owed at all
  • closeCompensation claims or legal advice

For these, call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915.
