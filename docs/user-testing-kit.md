# Independent phone testing and problem interviews

Prepared 6 October 2026. No participants or results are recorded yet. The owner plans to test
with people later. Use two people who have not seen Nivaran; do not coach them through the task.
Ask permission to take anonymous notes. Do not collect real bank records or private complaints.

## Ten-minute phone session

Use the local improvement build until it is deployed. On the public app, these changes will
appear only after a new release. Use fictional Saved runs; these tasks spend no model quota.

1. From Home, find the recorded case with two refund amounts. Ask: “What happened in this case?”
2. Find both refund sources. Ask: “Which amount would you choose, and what supports it?” Do not
   imply that answering is possible in read-only replay. Note whether the participant notices
   the Saved run label and the recorded answer.
3. Ask what “Stated in document”, “Your statement” and “Conflicting” mean. Record their words.
4. Find the plan, explain the next step and identify one date and its basis. Ask whether this
   is legal advice or whether the app sends a complaint.
5. Find the complaint, explain what still needs review, and find the printable pack if offered.
6. Open the already-complained Saved run. Ask what failed and what the record establishes.

For actual editing, open the labelled invented guided tour. Choose an amount, approve its
practice plan, then explicitly prepare the practice complaint. Add FAKE123456, explain its
flag, remove it and save the edit. This runs the real renderer/editor/linter in page memory,
with no provider or database call. It is not a live agent investigation or a saved case.

For each task record start/end time, success without help, assistance and the first point of
confusion. A saved replay is not evidence that someone completed a live upload or edit journey.
An approved live smoke/test session is a separate activity, with its own model budget.

## Five-minute problem interview

Ask before showing the app, so the interface does not lead the answers:

- Have you had an online refund promised but not received? When and what happened?
- Which documents/messages did you need, and how did you keep them?
- What was the hardest next action? What did you actually do?
- Did you abandon the follow-up? Why? What time or effort can you recall?
- After showing Nivaran, which part would help and which would you distrust?

Record collection date, recruitment method, participant count and whether quotes may be used.
Allow “cannot remember”; distinguish recalled estimates from timed tasks. Do not promise
recovery or imply the app resolved a real complaint. Add actual notes to problem-evidence.md
only after checking consent and removing identifying details.

## Separate owner checks

Preview the calendar file in a native calendar app; confirm all-day dates without importing.
Check browser print/save-PDF pagination and copy text. Neither an automated accessibility
scan nor a scripted model test replaces these checks or independent user testing.
